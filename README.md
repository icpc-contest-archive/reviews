# Reviews of the ICPC contest archive

The [ICPC contest archive](https://icpc-contest-archive.github.io/contests/) lists every ICPC contest from which a team could
advance toward a World Finals, with its results, its website and its problems. This repository is where people who know a
contest tell the archive what is right, what is wrong and what is missing.

## How to send a review

You do not write here directly. You review on the archive site, and the site hands your review to a form in this repository.

1. Open the archive in review mode: <https://icpc-contest-archive.github.io/contests/?review=1>
2. Go to a contest you know. Every fact on the page has a ✓ to confirm it and a ✎ to correct it, and every list has a + to add
   what is missing: a source, a file, a date, a place.
3. Go on to as many pages as you like. Your marks stay in your browser.
4. Press **Review** in the header, then **Submit review**. GitHub's form opens with your review filled in. Choose your
   connection to the contests and press Submit. A very large review is copied and pasted into the form instead; the site says
   so and does the copying.

You need a GitHub account, which is free.

## What happens to a review

Each review becomes one issue here. The maintainer checks every item against the sources and answers on the issue, so GitHub
notifies you.

- A **confirmation** is recorded with the fact.
- A **correction** changes the archive when the evidence holds.
- An **addition**, such as the address of a scoreboard or a problem set file, is checked, added and archived.

Review mode is new and in a pilot. Reviews sent now are handled by hand.

## Public

Issues here are public, and so are files attached to them. Your GitHub name is shown on the archive site beside what you
contribute. Please leave out private information, and attach only files you may share.

The archive's data and its site are in [icpc-contest-archive/contests](https://github.com/icpc-contest-archive/contests).
